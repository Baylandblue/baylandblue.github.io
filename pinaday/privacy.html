PinADay – Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026 · Bayland Blue LLC

PinADay ("the app") is made by Bayland Blue LLC. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics.
  • Your countdowns, pictures and settings are stored on your device. Bayland Blue LLC does not receive them.
  • The app does not send your countdowns or photos anywhere.
  • The optional one-time Unlock purchase is handled entirely by Google Play (or the App Store).
  • Bayland Blue LLC does not collect, receive or sell your data, except a bug report you choose to email us yourself.

Information stored on your device

What you enter is kept in the app's private storage on your device: each countdown's name, date and time, time zone, color, picture and other settings, and the app's own settings. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

Android backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account, so it can come back when you set up a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by Bayland Blue LLC, and you can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Your photos

If you choose your own photo for a countdown, you pick it with your phone's photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you select, not your other photos. The app keeps a small square copy of that photo in its private storage to show with the countdown and on the Photo widget. It never leaves your device. A copy you no longer use is deleted the next time the app starts; when you delete a countdown, its photo is kept for 30 days so the countdown can be brought back, and then deleted.

The home-screen widgets

If you add a widget, it shows the countdown's name, the time left and, for the Photo widget, its picture on your home screen, where anyone looking at your phone can see them.

Bug reports

To help find bugs, the app keeps a short log on your device of what it did: screens opened, counts, and technical errors, along with the app version, Android version and language. It contains no countdown names, dates, photos or anything else you typed, and old entries are deleted automatically. The log never leaves your device on its own. Settings → Send bug report copies it to your clipboard and opens your email app with it in a message to Bayland Blue LLC; it is sent only if you send that email, and then we receive the log and your email address. We use bug reports only to find and fix problems, and we delete one when you ask.

Purchases

The Unlock is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play (or, on iPhone and iPad, the App Store). The store handles your payment details, and Bayland Blue LLC never sees them. The store tells the app only whether you own the Unlock, so it can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall. Your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service (or Apple's terms). Refunds are handled by the store.

Ratings

After you've used the app for a while, it may ask Google Play (or the App Store) to show its rating window. The store shows it, and any rating or review you give goes to the store under its terms; the app and Bayland Blue LLC don't receive it.

Children

The app is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. Bayland Blue LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Bayland Blue LLC does not hold personal data about you, apart from any bug report you email us, which we delete on request. Your data in the app is on your device, under your control: you can edit or delete any of it in the app, or remove it all by uninstalling.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
