PinADay

Count down to your big day, right on your home screen. For Android phones and tablets, coming soon to Google Play.

PinADay is a countdown widget for the dates that matter: a birthday, retirement, a wedding, a baby's due date, a trip, or any day you choose, with a time if you like. Pin it to your home screen and it counts down by itself, every day, right through daylight-saving changes and travel, and ticks live through the last day.

What it does

  • A countdown on your home screen that updates itself at midnight, ticks live through the last day and says "Today!" when it arrives
  • Three widget styles: small, wide with a progress bar, and photo, with the countdown over a picture
  • Presets for birthdays, anniversaries, retirement, weddings, due dates and trips; birthdays and anniversaries roll on to next year by themselves
  • The time left your way: years, months and days; days; weeks and days (handy for a due date); days, hours and minutes; or hours
  • Colors and pictures: eight colors and eleven built-in pictures, or your own photo
  • Time zones for an event somewhere else, and correct through daylight-saving changes
  • 11 languages

Free, with one optional purchase

PinADay is free for one countdown in one small widget, with your own name, date and time. A one-time Unlock, with no subscription and no ads ever, adds unlimited countdowns and widgets, the wide and photo widgets, presets, units, colors, pictures and your own photo, yearly repeats and time zones.

Your countdowns stay yours

There's no account to make and no sign-in. Your countdowns and photos stay on your phone, and Bayland Blue LLC never receives them. See the privacy policy.
