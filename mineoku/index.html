Mineoku

A naval minefield logic puzzle for Android. Free on Google Play.

Get it on Google Play Watch gameplay

Chart the minefield by pure logic. Every board has exactly one solution, and you never have to guess: each mine can be found by careful deduction.

How it works

The board is split into colored regions. Place mines so that:

  • every row has exactly one mine,
  • every column has exactly one mine,
  • every region has exactly one mine, and
  • no two mines touch, not even diagonally.

Mark the cells you've ruled out with an X, or drag along a row or column to mark several at once. But careful: put an X on a real mine and it goes off. You have three lives per board. A misplaced mine never costs a life; it just shows up in red when it breaks a rule.

Each level is graded by the deductions it needs, so the challenge climbs steadily from the shallows to the deep.

Daily Sonar Op and Free Play

A new board every day, the same for every player, growing from 7×7 early in the week to 10×10 on Sunday, so keep your streak going: reaching 5, 10, 25, 50 or 100 days in a row (and every 25 after that) sets off a fanfare. Or pick any size from 6×6 to 10×10 in Free Play for a fresh board whenever you like.

Learn as you go

  • Boot Camp: five short coached drills teach the rules and the key deductions
  • An illustrated How to Play guide, available from any board
  • Earn up to three stars per level by beating par
  • Undo and redo, plus optional automatic X marks for the cells a new mine rules out

Help when you're stuck

Send a minesweeper to Sweep a mine into view, launch a Survey drone that shows and explains the next logical step, or Ping the board to clear a few safe cells. Helps never cost stars. Each has three charges, a used charge comes back 12 hours later, and you can watch an optional ad to get one back sooner.

A living coastline

Every board floats on open sea with a scene along the shore above it: a palm-fringed lagoon, a busy container harbor, a city waterfront, a clifftop lighthouse, a carrier battle group under way, and a mine countermeasures task force at work. Each sector has its own, the Daily changes with the date, and you can pin a favorite in Settings.

  • Swaying moored mines with blinking lights and sonar pings
  • A convoy sails across every cleared board
  • An underwater ambient soundtrack

Play your way

  • Color symbols on the regions, for players who find some tints hard to tell apart
  • Turn the board's motion and the water effect off if you prefer a still board
  • Cloud save: sign in to Google Play Games to keep your progress on all your devices
  • In 11 languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Turkish

Ads

The game is free to play, with a banner ad below the board and a short ad between boards after every five minutes of play. A one-time Remove Ads purchase in Settings turns off the banner and the ads between boards for good, and helps recharge in 2 hours instead of 12. Ads that restore a help charge only play when you choose to watch one.
