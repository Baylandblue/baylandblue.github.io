Mineoku – Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Bayland Blue LLC

Mineoku ("the game") is made by Bayland Blue LLC. This policy explains what information the game uses, who receives it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts of its own and asks for no personal information.
  • Your progress, stars, help charges and settings are stored on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also kept in your Google account so a new phone or reinstall gets it back.
  • Bayland Blue LLC does not collect, receive or sell your data, except a bug report you choose to email us yourself.
  • The game shows ads from Unity Ads and Google AdMob, and these ad networks collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • An optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your level results, stars, best times, daily puzzle streak, help charges, tutorial progress, settings and whether you bought Remove Ads in a file on your device. This file is not sent to Bayland Blue LLC. Uninstalling the game deletes it, and Settings → Reset progress clears your progress.

Bug reports

To help find bugs, the game keeps a short log of recent play on your device: the screens and boards you opened, your results, and technical events such as ad, purchase and cloud save errors, along with your device model, Android version, screen size and language. It contains no account names, advertising IDs, payment details or other personal information, and old entries are deleted automatically. The log never leaves your device on its own. Settings → SEND BUG REPORT opens your email app with the log in a message to Bayland Blue LLC; it is sent only if you send that email, and then we receive the log and your email address. We use bug reports only to find and fix problems, and we delete one when you ask.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

On Android the game signs in to Google Play Games, if you use it, and keeps a copy of your game progress (level results, stars, best times, daily puzzle streak, help charges and tutorial progress) in your Google account using Play Games saved games. Your settings and purchases are not included. The copy is stored by Google, not by Bayland Blue LLC, and only this game on your devices can read it. Google Play Games receives your Play Games profile and the fact that you play the game, under the Google Privacy Policy. If you don't sign in, the game works the same and your progress stays only on your device. Settings → Reset progress also clears the cloud copy. You can delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

Advertising (Unity Ads and Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad below the puzzle board, a full-screen ad between boards after every few minutes of play, and offers optional ads you can choose to watch to get back a help charge. These ads are provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and Google AdMob (Google). Depending on the version of the game you have, ads come either directly from Unity Ads, or from Google AdMob, which may use mediation to choose for each ad between Google's own advertisers and Unity Ads; in that case both Google and Unity receive the information below for the ads they handle. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the ad networks and their advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads (for example, views and taps) and diagnostic information.

Bayland Blue LLC does not receive this information in a form that identifies you; we see only summary reports such as how many ads were shown. Each ad network's use of this information is governed by its own policies:

  • Unity Ads: the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play)

The game offers one optional purchase, Remove Ads, which turns off the banner ad and the ads between boards for good, and makes help charges come back sooner. The purchase is made through Google Play: Google handles your payment details, and Bayland Blue LLC never sees them. Google Play tells the game only whether you own Remove Ads, so it can turn those ads off and restore the purchase if you reinstall. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: in versions that use Unity Ads directly, the first time you play the game asks whether ads may be personalized using your device's advertising ID. Either answer shows the same number of ads, and the game passes your answer to Unity before any ad loads. In versions that use Google AdMob, the game shows Google's consent form where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states) before ads load. Either way, you can change your choice at any time with Settings → Privacy options.
  • Personalized ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, under your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu varies by device).
  • Optional ads: ads that restore a help charge only play when you choose to watch one. Help charges also come back on their own over time.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. Bayland Blue LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Bayland Blue LLC does not hold personal data about you, apart from any bug report you email us, which we delete on request. Data collected by the ad networks for advertising, and your cloud save, can be managed through the choices above and through your Google account and device settings. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have additional rights, which you can exercise with Unity or Google for advertising data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
