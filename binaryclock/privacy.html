Binary Clock – Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026 · Bayland Blue LLC

Binary Clock ("the app") is made by Bayland Blue LLC. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The app collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no permission to use the internet.
  • It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.
  • Your settings and widget styles stay on your device.
  • Bayland Blue LLC receives nothing from the app, except a bug report you choose to email us yourself.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps your settings (language and battery saver) and each widget's style (preset, color, size, hour format and captions) in its private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Android backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include these settings in your device backup in your Google account, so they can come back when you set up a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by Bayland Blue LLC, and you can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

The home-screen widget

The widget reads your phone's clock and draws the time on your device. To keep the seconds right, it uses Android's exact alarms to redraw itself every few seconds; in battery saver mode, once a minute. Nothing about this leaves your device.

Bug reports

To help find bugs, the app keeps a short log on your device of what it did: screens opened and technical errors, along with the app version, Android version and language. It contains no times, styles or anything you chose, and old entries are deleted automatically. The log never leaves your device on its own. Settings → Send bug report copies it to your clipboard and opens your email app with it in a message to Bayland Blue LLC; it is sent only if you send that email, and then we receive the log and your email address. We use bug reports only to find and fix problems, and we delete one when you ask.

Purchase (Google Play)

Binary Clock is a paid download from Google Play. Google handles your payment details, and Bayland Blue LLC never sees them. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

Children

The app is a general-audience clock and is not directed to children under 13. Bayland Blue LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone, through the app.

Your rights

Bayland Blue LLC does not hold personal data about you, apart from any bug report you email us, which we delete on request. Everything the app keeps is on your device, under your control: change it in the app, or remove it all by uninstalling.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
