Grid Untangle 3D – Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Bayland Blue LLC

Grid Untangle 3D ("the game") is made by Bayland Blue LLC. This policy explains what information the game uses, who receives it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts of its own and asks for no personal information.
  • Your progress, stars, booster charges and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Bayland Blue LLC does not collect, receive or sell your data, except a bug report you choose to email us yourself.
  • The game shows ads from Unity Ads, which collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • An optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your board results, stars, best times, daily puzzle streak, Free Play results, booster charges, which tips you've seen, your settings, your answer to the ad consent question, and whether you bought Remove Ads in a file on your device. This file is not sent to Bayland Blue LLC. Uninstalling the game deletes it, and Settings → Reset progress clears your progress.

Bug reports

To help find bugs, the game keeps a short log of recent play on your device: the screens and boards you opened, your results, and technical events such as ad and purchase errors, along with your device model, Android version, screen size and language. It contains no account names, advertising IDs, payment details or other personal information, and old entries are deleted automatically. The log never leaves your device on its own. Settings → SEND BUG REPORT opens your email app with the log in a message to Bayland Blue LLC; it is sent only if you send that email, and then we receive the log and your email address. We use bug reports only to find and fix problems, and we delete one when you ask.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game shows a banner ad below the puzzle board, a full-screen ad between boards after every few minutes of play, and offers optional ads you can choose to watch to get back a booster charge. These ads are provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Unity and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads (for example, views and taps) and diagnostic information.

Bayland Blue LLC does not receive this information in a form that identifies you; we see only summary reports such as how many ads were shown. Unity's use of this information is governed by the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play)

The game offers one optional purchase, Remove Ads, which turns off the banner ad and the ads between boards for good, makes booster charges come back sooner, and opens Custom Mix in Free Play. The purchase is made through Google Play: Google handles your payment details, and Bayland Blue LLC never sees them. Google Play tells the game only whether you own Remove Ads, so it can turn those ads off and restore the purchase if you reinstall. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: the first time you play, the game asks whether ads may be personalized using your device's advertising ID. Either answer shows the same number of ads, and the game passes your answer to Unity before any ad loads. You can change your choice at any time with Settings → Privacy options.
  • Personalized ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, under your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu varies by device).
  • Optional ads: ads that restore a booster charge only play when you choose to watch one. Booster charges also come back on their own over time.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. Bayland Blue LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Bayland Blue LLC does not hold personal data about you, apart from any bug report you email us, which we delete on request. Data collected by Unity for advertising can be managed through the choices above and through your device settings. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have additional rights, which you can exercise with Unity for advertising data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds cloud saves or another ad network, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before that version is released.
