ReDue – Delete Your Account

ReDue by Bayland Blue LLC

ReDue only has an account if you signed in (with Google, or with Apple on iPhone and iPad), to sync across devices or to share tasks with other people. If you never did either, there is no account and nothing of yours in the cloud to delete.

Delete it in the app

This is the quickest way, and it takes effect right away.

  1. Open ReDue and go to Settings → Sync.
  2. Tap Delete my account and synced data.
  3. Confirm, then sign in again when asked: with your Google account on Android, or with Apple on iPhone and iPad if your account uses Sign in with Apple.

Your tasks stay on the device you did this from, including tasks from shared lists, and it stops syncing.

Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, email support@baylandblue.com with the subject "Delete my ReDue account". Send it from the address you used to sign in to ReDue (your Google address, or the Apple one, including a private relay address), so we can tell that the account is yours. We will delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your ReDue account: your email address, name, profile picture and user ID, and ReDue's access to your Apple sign-in if you used it.
  • Everything of yours in the cloud: your tasks, lists and completion history, with their notes, numbers, costs and photos.
  • Your membership of any shared lists: you are taken out of them. A shared list you were the last person in is deleted with its photos.
  • The push tokens that let the app hear about changes to shared lists.

What is kept

We keep nothing of your synced data after deletion. In a shared list that other people are still in, what you finished there stays in its history with your name, so the list stays complete for them; they can delete those entries themselves. If you email us a request, we keep that email only as long as we need it to handle your request.

Three things are not part of your ReDue account, so this does not remove them:

  • Tasks already on your devices. Uninstalling the app removes those.
  • Your ReDue Pro purchase, which belongs to your Google Play or App Store account.
  • Your Google account or Apple Account itself.
