ReDue – Delete Your Account

ReDue by Bayland Blue LLC

ReDue only has an account if you signed in with Google to use sync across devices. If you never turned on sync, there is no account and nothing of yours in the cloud to delete.

Delete it in the app

This is the quickest way, and it takes effect right away.

  1. Open ReDue and go to Settings → Sync.
  2. Tap Delete my account and synced data.
  3. Confirm, then choose your Google account when asked.

Your tasks stay on the device you did this from, and it stops syncing.

Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, email support@baylandblue.com with the subject "Delete my ReDue account". Send it from the Google address you used to sign in to ReDue, so we can tell that the account is yours. We will delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your ReDue account: your email address, name, profile picture and user ID.
  • Everything of yours in the cloud: your tasks, lists and completion history, with their notes, numbers, costs and photos.

What is kept

We keep nothing of your synced data after deletion. If you email us a request, we keep that email only as long as we need it to handle your request.

Three things are not part of your ReDue account, so this does not remove them:

  • Tasks already on your devices. Uninstalling the app removes those.
  • Your ReDue Pro purchase, which belongs to your Google Play account.
  • Your Google account itself.

See the privacy policy for more.
