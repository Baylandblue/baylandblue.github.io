ReDue – Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026 · Bayland Blue LLC

ReDue ("the app") is made by Bayland Blue LLC. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You can use the app without an account, and without giving any personal information.
  • Your tasks, lists, history, notes, numbers, photos and settings are stored on your device.
  • Sync across devices is optional. Only if you sign in to use it (with Google, or with Apple on iPhone and iPad), your tasks, lists and history (with notes, numbers and photos) are also stored in the cloud, under your account, so your devices can share them. You can delete that account and data at any time.
  • Sharing is optional too. If you share a task or list with your household, or join one, the people in it see its tasks and history, your name, and what you finish there.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking.
  • Bayland Blue LLC does not sell your data or use it for advertising. We receive data only if you turn on sync or sharing, or email us a bug report yourself.
  • An optional one-time ReDue Pro purchase is handled entirely by Google Play on Android, or the App Store on iPhone and iPad.

Information stored on your device

Everything you put in the app is kept in a database in the app's private storage on your device: your tasks and lists, when you completed them, and any notes, numbers, costs and photos you add, plus your streaks, badges and settings. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Photos. When you add a photo to a completion, the app opens your camera or lets you pick a picture, then keeps a scaled-down copy in its own storage. It does not read other photos on your device. Photos are uploaded only if you turn on sync.

Android backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account, so it can come back when you set up a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by Bayland Blue LLC, and you can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

iPhone and iPad backup. Your device's iCloud Backup, or a backup to your computer, may include the app's data so it can come back on a new device. Apple makes and stores that backup under the Apple Privacy Policy, not Bayland Blue LLC, and you control it in your device's settings.

Backups and exports you make

Settings → Back up creates a file with your data (including photos), and Export history creates a spreadsheet file of your completions. These files go only where you choose: a folder on your device or in Files, or an app you share them with, such as Google Drive, iCloud Drive or email. What happens to them there is up to you and that service.

Reminders and the home-screen widget

Reminders are scheduled on your device by Android's or iOS's notification system; nothing about them is sent to a server. (For shared lists, see "Push messages" below.) Notifications and the optional home-screen widget show task names on your screen, including the lock screen if your device's settings allow it.

Bug reports

To help find bugs, the app keeps a short log on your device of what it did: the screens opened, the kinds of actions taken (such as "completed" or "deleted"), how many reminders were scheduled, and technical errors, along with the app version, operating system version and language. It contains no task names, notes, numbers, photos or other content you entered, and old entries are deleted automatically. The log never leaves your device on its own. Settings → Send bug report copies it to your clipboard and opens your email app with it in a message to Bayland Blue LLC; it is sent only if you send that email, and then we receive the log and your email address. We use bug reports only to find and fix problems, and we delete one when you ask.

Sync across devices (optional)

In versions of the app that include it, ReDue Pro can keep your devices in step through Settings → Sync. Nothing leaves your device for this unless you sign in there: with Google, or on iPhone and iPad with Google or Apple. If you do:

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google, your Google account gives the app your email address, name and profile picture. If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives your email address, or a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email, and your name if you choose to share it; if it doesn't, the app asks you for a name to show in shared lists. Either way a user ID is created for you. These are used to sign you in and show which account is syncing. If your Apple and Google sign-ins share the same email address, they lead to the same ReDue account.
  • What is synced. Your tasks, lists and completion history, including the notes, numbers, costs and photos you added to them. Your settings stay on each device.
  • Where it is kept. On Google Cloud, using Google's Firebase services (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), in the United States (Iowa). Firebase handles this data for Bayland Blue LLC under the Firebase privacy terms. To run the service, Google also processes technical details such as your IP address.
  • Protection. Data is encrypted on its way to and from the cloud and while stored there, and the app can reach only the signed-in user's own data and the shared lists they belong to. Bayland Blue LLC runs this service, so it is technically able to reach the data. We look at it only if you ask for help, or if the law requires it.
  • Not shared. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone apart from the service providers named above, and, for a shared list, the people you share it with.
  • How long it is kept. Until you delete it. Removing a photo from a completion also removes it from the cloud. Signing out, uninstalling the app or no longer having ReDue Pro stops syncing but does not delete what is in the cloud.
  • Deleting it. Settings → Sync → Delete my account and synced data removes your account and everything of yours in the cloud, and, if you signed in with Apple, also ends ReDue's access to your Apple sign-in. Your tasks stay on your device. If you no longer have the app, you can ask us to delete it.

Purchases (Google Play or the App Store)

ReDue Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play on Android, or the App Store on iPhone and iPad. The store handles your payment details, and Bayland Blue LLC never sees them. The store tells the app only whether you own ReDue Pro, so it can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall; Restore purchase on the Pro screen asks the store again. A purchase on one store doesn't carry over to the other. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service, and Apple's by the Apple Privacy Policy and the App Store terms. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.

Sharing with your household (optional)

In versions of the app that include it, you can share a single task or a whole list with other people, such as your household, and take turns. Sharing needs ReDue Pro; joining someone else's list is free. Both need a sign-in, with Google or (on iPhone and iPad) Apple, which works as described under "Sync" above. Nothing is shared unless you share something or accept an invite.

  • What the other people in a shared list see. Its tasks and lists, their complete history including notes, numbers, costs and photos, whose turn it is, a tally of who finished how many, and your name (from your Google or Apple account, or the one you entered) next to the things you finish. They do not see your email address or anything outside the shared list.
  • Where it is kept. A shared list and everything in it is stored in the cloud the same way as synced data (Google Firebase, United States), and only its members can reach it, whether they use Android or iPhone. Your own tasks stay private unless you move them into a shared list.
  • Invites. An invite is a link with a code that anyone holding it can use to join, so send it only to people you mean to invite. It stops working after 7 days. Opening the link in a browser shows a page on baylandblue.com; the code is in the part of the link that browsers do not send to the website.
  • Leaving. Anyone can leave a shared list at any time, and its tasks then leave their device. Things they finished while in it stay in the list's history, with their name, for the people still in it. When the last person leaves, the list and its photos are deleted from the cloud, and that person keeps its tasks as their own.
  • Deleting your account. Deleting your account (see "Sync") also takes you out of every shared list. The history you added to lists other people are still in stays there, as above, so it stays complete for them.

Push messages for shared lists

So your phone hears about a change in a shared list (for example, that it's now your turn) even while the app is closed, the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service, which on iPhone and iPad delivers through Apple's Push Notification service. While you are signed in, the app stores a push token for your device (an identifier Google, and on iPhone and iPad Apple, issues for the app on that device) under your account. When a task in a shared list changes, a function run by Bayland Blue LLC on Google Cloud sends a message to the devices of the list's members. The message contains only an identifier for the list, never task names or personal details; your device then fetches the change itself and sets its own reminders. Your token is removed when you sign out or delete your account, and tokens that stop working are deleted automatically.

Children

The app is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. Bayland Blue LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Bayland Blue LLC holds personal data about you only if you turn on sync or sharing, or email us a bug report, which we delete on request. Your data in the app is under your control: you can edit or delete any of it in the app, or remove what is on your device by uninstalling. If you use sync, you can delete your account and synced data in the app or by asking us. You can also ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or to correct it, at the address below.
