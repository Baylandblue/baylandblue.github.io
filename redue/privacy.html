ReDue – Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Bayland Blue LLC

ReDue ("the app") is made by Bayland Blue LLC. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts and asks for no personal information.
  • Your tasks, lists, history, notes, numbers, photos and settings are stored on your device. Bayland Blue LLC does not receive them.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking.
  • Bayland Blue LLC does not collect, receive or sell your data, except a bug report you choose to email us yourself.
  • An optional one-time ReDue Pro purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

Everything you put in the app is kept in a database in the app's private storage on your device: your tasks and lists, when you completed them, and any notes, numbers, costs and photos you add, plus your streaks, badges and settings. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Photos. When you add a photo to a completion, the app opens your camera or lets you pick a picture, then keeps a scaled-down copy in its own storage. It does not read other photos on your device, and photos are never uploaded by the app.

Android backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account, so it can come back when you set up a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by Bayland Blue LLC, and you can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Backups and exports you make

Settings → Back up creates a file with your data (including photos), and Export history creates a spreadsheet file of your completions. These files go only where you choose: a folder on your device, or an app you share them with, such as Google Drive or email. What happens to them there is up to you and that service.

Reminders and the home-screen widget

Reminders are scheduled on your device by Android's notification system; nothing is sent to a server. Notifications and the optional home-screen widget show task names on your screen, including the lock screen if your device's settings allow it.

Bug reports

To help find bugs, the app keeps a short log on your device of what it did: the screens opened, the kinds of actions taken (such as "completed" or "deleted"), how many reminders were scheduled, and technical errors, along with the app version, Android version and language. It contains no task names, notes, numbers, photos or other content you entered, and old entries are deleted automatically. The log never leaves your device on its own. Settings → Send bug report copies it to your clipboard and opens your email app with it in a message to Bayland Blue LLC; it is sent only if you send that email, and then we receive the log and your email address. We use bug reports only to find and fix problems, and we delete one when you ask.

Purchases (Google Play)

ReDue Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles your payment details, and Bayland Blue LLC never sees them. Google Play tells the app only whether you own ReDue Pro, so it can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

Future features

Syncing across devices and sharing tasks with your household are planned for a later version. They will be optional, and this policy will be updated to describe them before they are released.

Children

The app is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. Bayland Blue LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Bayland Blue LLC does not hold personal data about you, apart from any bug report you email us, which we delete on request. Your data in the app is on your device, under your control: you can edit or delete any of it in the app, or remove it all by uninstalling.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
