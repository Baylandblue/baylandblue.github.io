401k Glance – Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Bayland Blue LLC

401k Glance ("the app") is made by Bayland Blue LLC. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts of its own, never connects to your retirement plan, and never asks for your plan's login.
  • Your holdings, balances and settings are stored on your device. Bayland Blue LLC does not receive them.
  • To get prices, the app sends the symbols of your funds to public market-data services. It never sends your balances or how much you hold.
  • The free version shows a banner ad from Unity Ads, which collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • If you turn on Premium's automatic backup, a copy of your data is kept in your own Google Drive, in a private app folder.
  • The app has no analytics. Bayland Blue LLC does not collect, receive or sell your data, except a bug report you choose to email us yourself.
  • The optional one-time Premium purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

What you enter is kept in the app's private storage on your device: your accounts and their names, your funds, share or unit counts and prices, your statement dates, your settings and, if you add one, your Finnhub key. The app also keeps the balances it has recorded over time and recent prices. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

Android backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account, so it can come back when you set up a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by Bayland Blue LLC, and you can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Getting prices

The app downloads prices directly from these services, both when you open it and in the background. Nothing passes through Bayland Blue LLC.

  • tsp.gov: if you have a Thrift Savings Plan account, the app downloads the public fund price file, which is the same for everyone.
  • Yahoo Finance: the app requests prices for the symbols of your funds, and the market funds used to estimate them. A symbol you type is looked up when you save it. See the Yahoo Privacy Policy.
  • Finnhub (optional): if you add your own Finnhub key for live estimates, the app sends that key and the symbols it needs a quote for to Finnhub. See the Finnhub Privacy Policy.

These services receive the symbols requested and, like any website, your device's IP address. They do not receive your balances, your share or unit counts, or your name.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The free version shows a banner ad. It has no full-screen ads. These ads are provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Unity and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads (for example, views and taps) and diagnostic information.

Unity does not receive your holdings or balances from the app. Bayland Blue LLC does not receive this information in a form that identifies you; we see only summary reports such as how many ads were shown. Unity's use of this information is governed by the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: the app asks whether ads may be personalized using your device's advertising ID. Either answer shows the same number of ads, and the app passes your answer to Unity before any ad loads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings.
  • Personalized ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, under your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu varies by device).
  • No ads: Premium removes ads.

Backup files you make

Settings → Back up creates a file with your accounts, holdings, recorded balances and settings, including your Finnhub key if you added one. The file is not encrypted. It goes only where you choose: a folder on your device, or an app you share it with, such as email. What happens to it there is up to you and that service, so keep it somewhere private.

Automatic backup to Google Drive (Premium, optional)

If you turn this on, the app asks you to sign in with Google and to allow it to use its own app folder in your Google Drive. It then saves the same contents as a backup file there about once a week, when you open the app, and keeps the last 8.

  • The app has access only to the private folder it creates. It cannot see or change anything else in your Drive, and the folder isn't visible among your Drive files.
  • The app talks directly to Google. Bayland Blue LLC does not receive your backups, your Google account details or your sign-in.
  • The backups are stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Turning the option off stops new backups. Backups already in your Drive stay there until you remove them: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps, find 401k Glance and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also revoke the app's access in your Google account.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data from your Drive is used only to restore your backups in the app. It is not used for ads, and it is not shared with anyone.

Sharing a picture of the screen

The Share option makes a picture of what's on your screen, including your balance, and hands it to the app you pick. Nothing is sent unless you choose where it goes.

The home-screen widget

If you add the widget, it shows your balance on your home screen, where anyone looking at your phone can see it.

Bug reports

To help find bugs, the app keeps a short log on your device of what it did: screens opened, counts, and technical errors, along with the app version, Android version and language. It contains no balances, share counts, fund names, your Finnhub key or anything else you typed, and old entries are deleted automatically. The log never leaves your device on its own. Settings → Send bug report copies it to your clipboard and opens your email app with it in a message to Bayland Blue LLC; it is sent only if you send that email, and then we receive the log and your email address. We use bug reports only to find and fix problems, and we delete one when you ask.

Purchases (Google Play)

Premium is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles your payment details, and Bayland Blue LLC never sees them. Google Play tells the app only whether you own Premium, so it can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

Children

The app is a general-audience finance app and is not directed to children under 13. Bayland Blue LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Bayland Blue LLC does not hold personal data about you, apart from any bug report you email us, which we delete on request. Your data in the app is on your device, under your control: you can edit or delete any of it in the app, or remove it all by uninstalling.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
