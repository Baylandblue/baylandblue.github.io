Literary Quote Clock Widget – Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026 · Bayland Blue LLC

Literary Quote Clock Widget ("the app") is made by Bayland Blue LLC. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The app collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no permission to use the internet.
  • It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • Bayland Blue LLC receives nothing from the app. If you email us, we receive that email.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps your settings in its private storage on your device: the widget's paper theme and text size, 12- or 24-hour time, whether to show the digital time stamp and the book and author, whether to highlight the time phrase, and whether to open each minute on a random passage. The widget also remembers which of the current minute's passages you last cycled to. The library of passages is built into the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Android backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include these settings in your device backup in your Google account, so they can come back when you set up a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by Bayland Blue LLC, and you can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

The home-screen widget

The widget reads your phone's clock and shows a passage for the current minute, updating itself once a minute. It needs no special permission, sets no alarms you can hear and shows no notifications. Nothing about this leaves your device.

Sharing a passage

When you tap Share, the app hands the passage text, with its book and author, to Android's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. It is sent only to the app you pick, under that app's own privacy policy. Bayland Blue LLC receives nothing.

Purchase (Google Play)

Literary Quote Clock Widget is a paid download from Google Play. Google handles your payment details, and Bayland Blue LLC never sees them. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

Children

The app is a general-audience clock and is not directed to children under 13. Bayland Blue LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone, through the app.

Your rights

Bayland Blue LLC does not hold personal data about you, apart from any email you send us, which we delete on request. Everything the app keeps is on your device, under your control: change it in the app, or remove it all by uninstalling.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
